Evidence Engine for macOS
=========================

To install
----------
1. Drag "Evidence Engine" onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Evidence Engine.


The first time you open it
--------------------------
macOS will say it cannot check Evidence Engine for malicious software. That is
because this build is not signed by an Apple-registered developer, not because
anything is wrong with it. You only have to get past this once.

  1. Double-click Evidence Engine in Applications and click OK on the message.
  2. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Click "Open Anyway" next to Evidence Engine, then confirm. You may be asked
     for your password or Touch ID.

On older versions of macOS you may instead be able to Control-click (or
right-click) Evidence Engine, choose Open, and click Open in the dialog.

After that it opens normally from the Dock or Applications like any other app.
Everything it needs is inside it, so there is nothing else to install.


What happens on first launch
----------------------------
It sets itself up, which takes a few minutes on a normal connection:

  - builds its own private Python environment from files inside the app, with
    no internet needed for that part
  - installs Ollama and downloads the local AI model, about 4 GB

Everything after that starts in a few seconds. The app window opens by itself
when it is ready. Closing the window shuts the whole thing down.

No account, no sign-up and no API keys are needed. The default model runs on
your own machine, so your articles and decisions are not sent anywhere.


Where your work is kept
-----------------------
In your home folder, in a folder called ".evidence-engine", NOT inside the app.
That holds your reviews, projects, screening decisions and cached article text.

Because of that you can replace the app with a newer version and keep
everything: drag the new one into Applications over the old one.


To uninstall
------------
Drag Evidence Engine from Applications to the Trash. To remove your reviews as
well, also delete the ".evidence-engine" folder in your home folder. (In Finder,
press Command-Shift-G and enter ~/.evidence-engine to find it.)
